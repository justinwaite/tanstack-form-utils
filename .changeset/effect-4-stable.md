Support the stable Effect 4 release and raise the minimum `effect` peer dependency to `>=4.0.0`. Effect `4.0.0` exports the HTTP modules from `effect/http` instead of `effect/unstable/http`, and the `/effect` entry point now imports from the new path. Effect `4.0.0-beta` and `4.0.0-rc` releases are no longer supported.
